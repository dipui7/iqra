AlifUp

Learn to read the Quran, letter by letter. Free, no ads, no account.

Open in your browser Download for Android or Windows Printable workbook (PDF)

What you'll learn

  • The 28 letters: their names, sounds and shapes — hear each one and trace it.
  • Vowels, sukūn, shadda, tanwīn and long vowels, practised on real words from Juz ʿAmma.
  • Memorise 21 short surahs, one ayah at a time.
  • Read any surah with the recitation, repeat ayat to memorise, tajweed colours and an English translation.

Installing on Android

  1. On your phone, open the download page and tap AlifUp-…apk.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask whether to allow installs from your browser or Files app — allow it, go back, and tap Install.
  3. To update later, install the new APK the same way. Your progress is kept.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the web version in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. AlifUp gets its own icon and opens full screen, like an app.
  3. Your progress is saved in the browser on that device. To move it, use Settings → Backup.

Installing on Windows

  1. Download AlifUp-…msi from the same page and open it.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info → Run anyway (the installer isn't signed by a paid certificate).
