AlifUp teaches adult beginners to read the Quran, step by step, with short lessons, real Quran text and recitation.

<b>Learn to read</b>
• The 28 Arabic letters: their names, sounds and four shapes. Tap a letter to hear it, and trace it with your finger.
• Short vowels, then sukūn, shadda, tanwīn and long vowels — practised on real words from Juz ʿAmma.
• Unit checks and exams, including questions where you recite.

<b>Memorise</b>
• 21 short surahs, from Al-Fātiḥah to An-Nās: listen to each ayah, then practise what comes next, the next word, and putting the ayat in order.

<b>Quran reader</b>
• Every surah, with the recited ayah highlighted.
• Repeat an ayah or loop a range of ayat for memorising.
• Tajweed colours and an English translation.
• Ayat you've heard play again offline.

<b>Keep going</b>
• Practice sessions for what you find hard, daily goal, streaks, badges and an optional daily reminder.

<b>Free and private</b>
No ads, no account, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone. Voice recordings are compared on the device and never uploaded.
