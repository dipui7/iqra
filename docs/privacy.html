AlifUp privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: AlifUp collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress — lessons completed, XP, streaks, review history, coins, settings — is saved in a file on your phone or computer. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Recitations you have played are kept in the app's cache so they play again without internet. You can clear them by clearing the app's cache.

The microphone

Some exam questions ask you to recite. Only then, and only after you allow it, the app records your voice. The recording is compared with the reciter's recording on your device, kept only in memory while the question is open, and then discarded. It is never saved to a file and never uploaded.

Internet

To play recitation, the app downloads audio files from everyayah.com. As with any website, their server sees your device's IP address when a file is downloaded; the app sends nothing else. Links on the About and "Help others" pages open in your browser only when you tap them.

Notifications (Android)

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules it on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

AlifUp collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/dipui7/iqra/issues.
